# Communication and style

#### Style

To aim for consistency of style in all _UJ_ documentation, a short style guide for the GitBook has been posted [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10aooH\_YCVX\_\_pXFqnY1l8Kn2\_DPX9wdHdR9AfImSuDs/edit?usp=sharing). Feel free to suggest changes or additions using the comments. \
Note this document, like so many, is under construction and likely to change without notice. The plan is to make use of it for any outward-facing communications.

## Describing our contributions to other initiatives

Alongside evaluations and Pivotal Questions, mention concrete contributions to related initiatives when relevant to an overview, funding update, or discussion of our wider influence. Use the [initiative descriptions and public sources](../parallel-partner-initiatives-and-resources/README.md#initiatives-we-contribute-to) to keep the roles and links consistent.

Suggested short copy:

> The Unjournal partners with Metaculus and Sentient Futures on the [Animal Futures forecasting tournament](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/), helping connect forecasts to animal-welfare research and decisions. We have also provided an example and advice for [The Alignment Journal](https://blog.alignmentjournal.org/adaptation-to-ai/), with David Reinstein coauthoring posts on its policies and use of AI.

For Animal Futures updates, link to the [public dashboard and short report](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/), identify them as preliminary and AI-prepared, and distinguish forecasts from resolved outcomes. For The Alignment Journal, describe the contribution as advice and shared experience; avoid implying that The Unjournal runs the journal. Use public articles rather than draft or editing links.
