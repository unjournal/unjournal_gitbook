Open discussion

Questions, suggestions, challenges: Zoom chat or just speak up.

Some starting questions

  • What would make a strong researcher take on a fast AI economics evaluation? Purpose, recognition, prizes, pay?
  • How should evaluators use AI? Should we run strong AI evaluations first and ask humans to check them, or keep them separate and compare?
  • AI governance work goes stale in weeks. How fast do we need to be, and what formats should we evaluate (dashboards, datasets, living reviews)?

We'll respond to every comment, including those we don't reach live, and add them to the record.

On the second question: our AI-vs-human comparison (60 papers) found the model caught about 80% of issues human evaluators agreed on: results. Gary King's recent "peerless" work is close to our planned rater study.
