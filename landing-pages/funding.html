Funding history

2022–present · Updated November 2, 2026 · All amounts in US dollars

The Unjournal commissions and publishes public expert evaluations of research relevant to global priorities. Philanthropic grants have supported this work since the initial pilot in 2022.

Approximately $796,000 in major grants awarded

Includes the $130,000 Lightcone Commons award announced on November 1, 2026.
