# Parallel/partner initiatives and resources

## Initiatives we contribute to

### Animal Futures forecasting tournament

The Unjournal partners with Metaculus and Sentient Futures on the [Animal Futures tournament](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/). We help connect forecasting questions to research and decisions about animal welfare through our [Pivotal Questions initiative](../pivotal-questions/README.md).

The questions cover alternative proteins, farmed and wild animal welfare, and the effects of advanced AI on animals. Our [public update and dashboard](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/) summarize forecasts and discussion, with links back to the questions and their resolution criteria. The update is preliminary and AI-prepared at David Reinstein's direction.

### The Alignment Journal

The Unjournal has provided an example and advice for [The Alignment Journal](https://www.alignmentjournal.org/), which focuses on the scientific foundations of understanding, predicting, and steering AI systems. David Reinstein contributed to its design discussions and coauthored public posts on [features and policies](https://blog.alignmentjournal.org/features-and-policies/) and [adaptation to AI](https://blog.alignmentjournal.org/adaptation-to-ai/).

The latter describes how The Unjournal's approach informed the journal's proposed AI-use policy and discusses our comparisons of human and AI evaluations. This is a concrete example of sharing experience from open research evaluation.

## Related initiatives

As part of _The Unjournal's_ general approach, we keep track of and maintain contact with other initiatives in open science, open access, robustness/transparency, and encouraging impactful research. We want to be coordinated. We want to partner with other initiatives and tools where there is overlap, and clearly explain where (and why) we differentiate from other efforts.

See our **[Related Initiatives Comparison](related-initiatives-comparison.md)** for a curated overview of initiatives in the same space, including:
- [COS LifeCycle Journal](https://lifecyclejournal.org/) - open review where Unjournal is an evaluation provider
- [Institute for Replication (I4R)](https://i4replication.org/) - robustness replication of top papers
- [Peer Communities In (PCI)](https://peercommunityin.org/) - diamond open access with recommender model

See also: [elife.md](elife.md "mention") and [peer-communities-in.md](peer-communities-in.md "mention")
