# Animal Futures: what the forecasts say so far

*Checked October 13, 2026 · [Full update and discussion](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/) · [Forecast on Metaculus](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/)*

The [Animal Futures tournament](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/) lists 21 questions. The original 16 now have 48–71 forecasters each (mean 54). Five added on September 20 have 19–27 each (mean 23), up from 15–23 on October 3. These are per-question counts, not unique participants. One question has resolved: a [new cultivated-meat product received regulatory approval](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43492/will-a-new-cultivated-meat-product-be-approved-before-july-2027/) following Aleph Farms’ Singapore approval; [commercial availability remains limited](https://gfi.org/resource/where-cultivated-meat-can-be-sold/).

Current forecasts put [2%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43163/will-plant-based-meat-reach-10-of-us-retail-meat-sales-before-2031/) on plant-based meat and seafood reaching 10% of US packaged-meat sales before 2031; [the 2025 share was 1.4%](https://gfi.org/resource/analyzing-plant-based-meat-and-seafood-sales/). Separately, they put [1%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43288/will-nueva-pescanovas-octopus-farm-be-approved-before-june-27/) on approval of Nueva Pescanova’s planned commercial octopus farm before June 2027, an outcome that would be a setback for animal welfare. The crowd puts about [80%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43329/how-many-more-us-states-will-ban-cultivated-meat-before-july-2027/) on at least one more US state cultivated-meat ban by July 2027, under the question’s definition. For Q1 2027, estimates are [43.6% for EU commercial layers](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43279/what-of-the-eu-hen-flock-will-be-in-ovo-sexed-in-2027/) and [2.8% for the US hen flock](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43280/what-of-the-us-hen-flock-will-be-in-ovo-sexed-in-2027/) to be sexed in ovo.

A new discussion raises a design question: the [ANIMA forecast comment](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43493/what-will-june-26-to-june-27-models-score-on-anima/#comments) notes that the [benchmark’s own page](https://compassionbench.com/ahb) now calls it legacy for distinguishing frontier models, while retaining value for open-source training. Organizers could check that the outcome remains measurable through its full window and still fits the intended decision. There are 35 comments across the questions; eight have none. I found no publicly documented funding or policy decision citing a tournament forecast in the sources checked. Only one question has resolved, so it is too early to judge accuracy.
