# Animal Futures: what the forecasts say so far

*Checked October 23, 2026 · [Full dashboard](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/)*

AI prepared this preliminary update at David Reinstein’s direction. The forecasts are from the Metaculus community.

The [Animal Futures tournament](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/) still has 21 questions and one resolved outcome. Participation is growing: the original 16 questions now average 56 forecasters each (49–74); the five September additions average 26 (23–31). These are per-question account counts, including some bots.

The broad forecast picture is steady. The crowd gives [2%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43163/) to plant-based meat and seafood reaching 10% of US retail packaged-meat dollar sales before 2031 ([1.4% in 2025](https://gfi.org/resource/analyzing-plant-based-meat-and-seafood-sales/)). Separately, it gives [1%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43288/) to Nueva Pescanova’s octopus farm being approved before June 2027. It puts roughly [80%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43329/) on another US state cultivated-meat ban before July 2027. For Q1 2027, in-ovo sexing estimates remain far apart: [43.7% of EU commercial layers](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43279/) and [2.96% of the US flock](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43280/).

One useful finding: a [Plant Based Cities Movement article](https://plantbasedcities.org/from-taboo-to-measurement-another-pattern-of-change/) cites the approximately $6.8 billion plant-based sales forecast as a possible benchmark for its institutional food work. That documents public engagement with a forecast. The article reports no resulting procurement, funding, or policy change. **Which forecast, at what threshold, would change a decision?** This remains the most useful question for organizers, funders, and advocates to answer.

A [new Mexico consumption comment](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43601/what-will-be-mexicos-forecasted-meat-consumption-for-2027/#comment-1182469) asks for resolution from a USDA release. The question is still open. Its outcome is the first available official forecast for 2027, rather than realized 2027 consumption; the proposed result still needs verification. There are now 36 comments across the questions, and eight questions have none.

The [cultivated-meat approval question](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43492/) remains the only officially resolved outcome. The [dashboard](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/#discussion) retains the ANIMA benchmark and resolution-definition discussions, plus suggestions for organizers. One resolution is too little to judge tournament accuracy.
