# Animal Futures: what the forecasts say so far

*Checked November 13, 2026 · [Full dashboard](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/)*

AI prepared this preliminary update at David Reinstein’s direction. The forecasts are from the Metaculus community.

The [Animal Futures tournament](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/33016/) has 21 questions and 1 officially resolved outcome. The original 16 questions now average 59 forecasting accounts each (52–80), up from 56 on October 23; the five September additions average 31 (27–35), up from 26. These are accounts per question, including some bots, rather than a count of distinct people.

The main forecast picture is steady. The crowd gives [2%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43163/) to plant-based meat and seafood reaching 10% of US retail packaged-meat dollar sales before 2031 ([1.4% in 2025](https://gfi.org/resource/analyzing-plant-based-meat-and-seafood-sales/)). It gives [1%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43288/) to Nueva Pescanova’s octopus farm being approved before June 2027, and roughly [82%](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43329/) to another US state cultivated-meat ban before July 2027. For Q1 2027, in-ovo sexing estimates remain far apart: [42% of EU commercial layers](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43279/) and [2.77% of the US flock](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43280/).

A [Plant Based Cities Movement article](https://plantbasedcities.org/from-taboo-to-measurement-another-pattern-of-change/) cites the approximately $6.8 billion plant-based sales forecast as a possible benchmark for its institutional food work. We found no documented change to a procurement, funding, or policy decision. **Which forecast, at what threshold, would change a decision?** We’d particularly like funders, advocates, and policymakers to name one.

The [cultivated-meat approval question](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43492/) remains the only official resolution. A [follow-up on Mexico’s consumption question](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/43601/what-will-be-mexicos-forecasted-meat-consumption-for-2027/#comment-1238855) asks for resolution, but it is still open and the proposed result remains unverified. The question predicts an official forecast for 2027, rather than actual 2027 consumption. Across the tournament there are 37 comments; 8 questions have none. The [dashboard](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/#discussion) covers the substantive discussions and [suggestions for organizers](https://uj-animal-futures-update.netlify.app/#suggestions). One resolution is too little to judge tournament accuracy.
